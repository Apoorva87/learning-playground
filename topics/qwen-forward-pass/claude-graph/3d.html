Drag to orbit. Shift-drag or right-drag to pan. Scroll to zoom. Click a sphere to inspect it. Labels give the block number and R (recurrent) or F (full attention). Winding the chain into a coil changes only where nodes are drawn: the same stage sits at the same angle on every turn, and the red path climbs through every turn in order.

Reading the picture

Going around one turn follows one block's dependency steps; going up is the strict layer-to-layer order. Spheres side by side at one angle (concentric) are ops that could run in parallel. Large spheres are the weight matrices (MLP in orange, attention projections in blue). The long run of small yellow spheres on recurrent turns is the recurrent core: a chain of tiny steps that each wait on the previous one. Counts are shape-derived estimates; see the 2D page for the method and limits.
