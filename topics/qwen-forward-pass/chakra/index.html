Read the evidence

PyTorch records a scope’s parent in ctrl_deps. Chakra’s standard converter traverses CPU calls in order and places predecessor IDs in data_deps for replay scheduling. The small export here assigns that field by exact tensor identity across sibling calls. Its region supplies the control parent, and its times are matched Kineto intervals rounded to integral microseconds.
